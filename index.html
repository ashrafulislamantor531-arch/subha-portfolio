About The Artist

A Lifelong Reverence for Water & Transparency

Hi, I'm Subha. For me, painting is not about documenting what is visible, but translating what is felt in the fleeting moments between shadows and illuminated skies.

Having honed the demanding disciplines of both spontaneous wet-on-wet gestures and meticulous dry-brush architectural line-work, my focus rests on capturing stillness: morning mist hovering over mountain valleys, the quiet glow of an autumn sanctuary, and the vibrant vitality of creatures great and small.
